# Individual Profile

**Name:** Laura M. Cha (查史美倫)
**Title:** Senior Advisor to the Board, Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited (HKEX); former Chairman, HKEX (April 2018 – April 2024)
**Company:** Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited (HKEX)

## 1. Career

- 1983–1990: Practiced corporate and securities law in San Francisco and Hong Kong.
- 1991–2000: Held senior roles at the Hong Kong Securities and Futures Commission (SFC), rising to Deputy Chairman in 1998.
- 2001–2004: Vice-Chairman, China Securities Regulatory Commission (CSRC) — the first person from Hong Kong, and the first non-mainlander, to hold a vice-ministerial-level position in the central government of the People's Republic of China.
- 2004–2022: Non-official Member, Executive Council of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region (HKSAR) Government.
- 2007–2012: Chairman, University Grants Committee of Hong Kong.
- 2013–2018: Chairman, Financial Services Development Council (FSDC), Hong Kong.
- April 2018 – April 2024: Chairman, Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited (HKEX) — the first woman to chair HKEX in its history.
- April 2024–present: Senior Advisor to the Board, HKEX, following the end of her chairmanship term.

# Company Profile

**Company:** Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited (HKEX)
**Stock Code:** HKG: 0388

## 1. Sector Classification

- Sector: Financial Services — Capital Markets / Financial Exchanges & Data.
- HKEX operates the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong (SEHK), Hong Kong Futures Exchange (HKFE), Hong Kong Securities Clearing Company (HKSCC), and the London Metal Exchange (LME), among other subsidiaries.

Sources: [HKEX Group – About HKEX, Organisation](https://www.hkexgroup.com/About-HKEX/Organisation?sc_lang=en)

## 2. Business Description

HKEX is the operator of Hong Kong's securities and derivatives markets and their related clearing houses. Its core businesses are Cash Market (equities trading and settlement), Equity and Financial Derivatives, Commodities (via the London Metal Exchange, acquired in 2012), and Platform and Infrastructure services, including Stock Connect links with mainland Chinese exchanges (Shanghai and Shenzhen). It also operates OTC Clearing Hong Kong and, since 2023, wholly owns the London Metal Exchange and LME Clear.

Sources: [HKEX Group 2024 Final Results Announcement](https://www.hkex.com.hk/-/media/HKEX-Market/News/News-Release/2025/250227news/250227_FY-Results_e.pdf)

## 3. Geographic Coverage

- **HQ:** Exchange Square, Central, Hong Kong SAR.
- **Global presence:** Hong Kong (headquarters and primary markets); London, United Kingdom (London Metal Exchange and LME Clear); mainland China (Stock Connect infrastructure and representative offices); additional representative/business development offices in cities including New York, London, Beijing, Shanghai and Singapore.

Sources: [HKEX Group – About HKEX](https://www.hkexgroup.com/About-HKEX?sc_lang=en)

## 4. Key Management

- **Chief Operating Officer and Chief Executive, Stock Exchange of Hong Kong / Hong Kong Futures Exchange:** Vanessa Lau (effective January 2025, succeeding Wilfred Yiu, who retired end of 2024).

## 8. Strategy and Future

HKEX's stated strategy centers on strengthening Hong Kong's role as the primary offshore gateway between mainland China and international capital, expanding Stock Connect products, deepening derivatives and commodities offerings via the LME, and pursuing growth in fixed income, currency and data/index businesses. Leadership continuity (CEO contract extended to 2030) signals an intent toward long-term strategic consistency.

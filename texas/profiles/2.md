## PART 2: COMPANY PROFILE

### About Sovereign Wealth Fund Institute (SWFI):
o SWFI is an independent, privately held American research, data, and intelligence organization that analyzes sovereign wealth funds, public pensions, family offices, insurers, and other long-term institutional asset owners; it has no PIF ownership relationship and operates as an independent third-party data provider covering PIF among other sovereign investors.
o SWFI's stated direction is to continue expanding its proprietary institutional-investor database and membership/data-subscription platform (SWFI Data Labs), broadening coverage into family offices and wealth management, and increasing engagement through research, events, and its Sovereign Wealth Quarterly publication.

Sources:

### 1. Sector Classification

o Sub-sector: Institutional Investor / Sovereign Wealth Fund Intelligence and Analytics

Sources:
- https://www.swfinstitute.org/

### 2. Business Description
o SWFI is a data, research, and intelligence firm that tracks and analyzes sovereign wealth funds, public pensions, central banks, family offices, and other institutional asset owners globally.
o Its products include the Linaburg-Maduell Transparency Index (a proprietary transparency-rating methodology for sovereign wealth funds, co-invented and owned by SWFI), the SWFI Data Labs institutional-investor database (reported to cover 600,000+ institutional entities), and the Sovereign Wealth Quarterly research publication, all developed and owned in-house.
o SWFI distributes its research and data through a tiered membership/subscription platform, direct data licensing, specialized consulting engagements, and industry conferences and networking events.
o Its primary revenue model is subscription and membership access to its data platform and research (including tiered membership plans introduced in 2023), supplemented by consulting services and event revenue.

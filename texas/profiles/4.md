### 1. Career

− Noel Tata currently leads Tata Trusts as Chairman while also holding chairmanships and vice-chairmanships across several Tata group operating companies, roles built over more than four decades within the Tata group.
  - o Expected to step down from operating-company boards (Trent, Voltas, Tata Investment Corporation, Tata International, Tata Steel) around November 2026 under the Tata Group's retirement-age policy for non-executive directors; this age limit does not apply to his Tata Trusts chairmanship.
  - o Since January 2025, confirmed with unlimited/lifetime tenure as Chairman of the Sir Dorabji Tata Trust and Sir Ratan Tata Trust.
  - o Since October 2024, Chairman of Tata Trusts (Sir Dorabji Tata Trust and Sir Ratan Tata Trust, and the other trusts constituting Tata Trusts), succeeding Ratan Tata following his death.
  - o Since March 2014, Chairman of Trent Limited; previously Managing Director of Trent from June 1999, overseeing its growth from a single store in 1998 to a multi-format retail chain of over 800 stores.
  - o From August 2010 to November 2021, Managing Director of Tata International Limited, growing the trading and distribution arm's turnover from approximately US$ 500 Mn to over US$ 3 Bn; continues as Chairman, Tata International.
  - o Since 2003, Director of Titan Industries (now Titan Company) and Voltas Limited; currently serves as Vice Chairman of both.
  - o Currently also Chairman of Voltas Limited and Tata Investment Corporation Limited.
  - o Associated with the Tata group for over 40 years across multiple group companies prior to and alongside the roles above.

## PART 2: COMPANY PROFILE

### About Tata Trusts

− Tata Trusts' identity and strategic positioning:
  - o Tata Trusts is a group of approximately 15 charitable/public trusts (not a commercial company) and is the controlling/promoter shareholder group of Tata Sons Private Limited, holding roughly 66% of its equity; current Chairman is Noel Tata (since October 2024), based at Bombay House, Mumbai. Tata Trusts is not owned by, or affiliated with, Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund.
  - o The Trusts are expected to continue funding India's largest philanthropic network while taking a more active governance role in Tata Sons and adapting to new trustee-tenure rules under the Maharashtra Public Trusts (Amendment) Ordinance, 2025.

### 2. Business Description

− Tata Trusts' structure and operating model:
  - o Tata Trusts comprises approximately 15 charitable/public trusts established over more than a century, beginning with the JN Tata Endowment founded by Jamsetji Tata in 1892; the two largest constituent trusts are the Sir Ratan Tata Trust (established 1918/1919) and the Sir Dorabji Tata Trust (established 1932).
  - o Products/brands (owned vs. licensed): N/A — not applicable to a charitable trust; Tata Trusts does not produce or license commercial products or brands.
  - o Go-to-market: Operates through grant-making, direct implementation, and co-partnership models across healthcare, education, livelihoods, nutrition, rural development, water/sanitation, social justice, skilling, environment, digital access, arts/culture, sports, and disaster relief; has seeded institutions including Tata Memorial Hospital, TIFR, TISS, and NCPA.
  - o Revenue model: Not revenue-generating in a commercial sense — the Trusts are funded primarily by dividends from their approximately 66% equity stake in Tata Sons Private Limited, which they deploy as philanthropic grants rather than retaining as profit.

### 6. Significant Events

− Institutional history, in chronological order:
  - o 1892: Jamsetji Tata establishes the JN Tata Endowment, the founding trust of what becomes Tata Trusts.
  - o 1918/1919: The Sir Ratan Tata Trust is established with a corpus of approximately ₹8 million.
  - o 1932: The Sir Dorabji Tata Trust is established.
  - o 1991: Ratan Tata becomes Chairman of Tata Trusts.
  - o 2022: Mehli Mistry is inducted as a trustee.
  - o 2024: Ratan Tata dies on 9 October.
  - o 2024: Noel Tata is appointed Chairman of all the Trusts on 11 October, succeeding Ratan Tata.
  - o 2025: Noel Tata is confirmed with unlimited/lifetime tenure as Chairman in January.
  - o 2025: The Maharashtra Public Trusts (Amendment) Ordinance is signed and takes effect between 30 August and 1 September, capping perpetual-trustee terms.
  - o 2025: Mehli Mistry steps down as trustee on 28 October amid a governance dispute.
  - o 2026: The Tata Sons board reappoints N. Chandrasekaran as Chairman by a 4-to-1 vote on 17 September, over Noel Tata's objection.
